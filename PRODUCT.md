# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

noClue partnership and research team members who need to find, qualify, and contact potential sponsors, venues, speakers, and community partners.

## Product Purpose

Turn permitted public-source research and deliberate contact enrichment into a reviewable list of outreach-ready people and organisations.

## Positioning

This is an evidence-first research desk: every useful lead can retain its public-source proof before a paid email or phone lookup is requested.

## Operating Context

Users create a research question, collect only approved public webpages, review and save records, then selectively use Apollo, RocketReach, or Surfe. Outreach remains a human action.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The product runs locally with a React interface and Express API. Provider keys are server-side only. Public collection respects allowed domains and robots rules. Paid providers are optional and must be chosen explicitly. Phone lookup results may be asynchronous.

## Brand Commitments

Product name: noClue Research. The requested interface is minimal and uses light blue and white only.

## Evidence on Hand

Local JSON storage at `data/store.json`; public web collector; records, evidence, drafts, enrichment history; Apollo and Surfe adapters. RocketReach is being added.

## Product Principles

- Explain what each action does before it costs money.
- Make public proof visible before contact enrichment.
- Keep the workflow small: learn, research, review saved data.
- Never send outreach automatically.
